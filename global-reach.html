Export Preservation Options

Export Preservation & Heavy-Duty Crating

Compressor spares such as cast iron cylinder liners, crankshafts, and valve plates can be sensitive to atmospheric moisture during ocean transit. Packaging and preservation requirements are confirmed for each shipment:

  • 🛡️ 1. Corrosion Protection: Protective coatings may be specified for suitable machined surfaces.
  • 📦 2. VCI Packaging: Available when appropriate and stated in the shipment arrangements.
  • 🌲 3. ISPM-15 Wood Packaging: Where required, treated by an approved method stated on the applicable certificate. Crating options are confirmed per shipment.
