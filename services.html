Case Study CS-01

600-Ton Ammonia Cold Storage Compressor Revamp

📍 Kutch, Gujarat • Large Seafood & Agro Freezing Facility | ⚙️ Grasso RC11 (8-Cylinder Reciprocating)

The Challenge:

Severe oil carryover into the evaporator coils, high discharge temperatures exceeding 135°C, and a 28% drop in refrigeration capacity during peak season.

Our Engineering Solution:

Conducted cylinder bore ovality inspection, replaced all 8 cylinder liners with plateau-honed alloy cast sleeves, installed conformable PTFE-faced ring packs, and renewed suction/discharge valve assemblies.

Verified Results:

Restored volumetric capacity to 98% of design baseline, lowered operating discharge temperature to 112°C, and cut facility power consumption by 14.5% within the first month.

Case Study CS-02

Emergency Marine Reefer Compressor Replacement in Port

📍 Nhava Sheva (JNPT), Mumbai • Container Cargo Vessel | ⚙️ Carrier 5H120 Marine HVAC Compressor

The Challenge:

Connecting rod bearing seizure occurred 36 hours prior to container vessel departure, threatening perishable refrigerated cargo containers.

Our Engineering Solution:

Efforts Engineers dispatched ready-stock forged connecting rods, crankshaft bush sets, and Swedish steel valve discs via priority courier within 6 hours. Provided technical guidance to shipboard chief engineer.

Verified Results:

Vessel completed compressor reassembly and trial run 8 hours ahead of departure schedule, averting costly demurrage fees.

Case Study CS-03

Reverse-Engineered Spares for Obsolete Process Compressor

📍 Middle East Industrial Petrochemical Complex | ⚙️ Sabroe Vintage SMC 180 Reciprocating Gas Unit

The Challenge:

OEM discontinued spare parts supply for this vintage gas compressor, causing a critical hydrogen-offgas process line shutdown.

Our Engineering Solution:

Received worn valve plates and piston pin samples. Carried out spectroscopic chemical composition analysis and CNC machined 12 sets in corrosion-resistant alloy steel with certified MTC 3.1 reports.

Verified Results:

The reverse-engineered components achieved over 18,000 continuous operating hours with zero failure incidents.
